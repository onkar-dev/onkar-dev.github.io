01 — About

I'm a Flutter mobile engineer based in Pune, India, currently building Verto Pay — a live cross-border remittance app processing $25B+ in transaction volume across 190+ countries — as part of a six-person engineering team. My focus is state management architecture — BLoC, clean architecture, and the kind of dependency structure that lets a fintech app change fast without breaking payment flows.

Before Verto, I spent years on stock broking and trading platforms at Greeksoft Technologies and Mplussoft Technologies — real-time data, order flows, and UI that has to stay correct under pressure. That background is why I default to defensive architecture: typed error handling, use-case boundaries, and dependency injection, rather than convenience shortcuts.

I'm looking for teams building serious financial products who want someone who's already lived inside the failure modes — not someone learning them on the job.
